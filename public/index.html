You are doing everything right and hearing nothing back

Here is the truth most people never get told. You are usually not being rejected because you are not good enough. You are being rejected because you are invisible, or because your application is filtered out before a human ever properly reads it, which we can help with.

A single advert today pulls hundreds of CVs, often thousands. Easy Apply and frictionless applications mean huge numbers of unsuitable people apply in two clicks, and the noise drowns the genuinely good candidates. To cope, companies lean on AI screening and recruiters giving each CV a flash glance. Your job is to survive that filter and rise out of the noise. That is exactly what Coderise Coaching does for you.

I am Andrew Basley. I have worked in recruitment and hiring for 15 years, as a Tech Recruiter and for six years as a Head of HR. Most career advice is written by people who have never shortlisted anyone. This is the opposite: the inside view, turned into practical help.
